Idea Tiles 1.3.1 introduces the native Mac workspace and Artifact Studio.

NEW
• Turn a board, branch, or selected tiles into reports, briefs, plans, diagrams, code scaffolds, SVG assets, static prototypes, or Image Playground artwork.
• Review, save, export, and trace artifacts back to their source tiles.
• Choose Apple Foundation Models, loopback Ollama, supported direct providers, or an authorized Dreamer profile.
• Optional cloud sync keeps images local unless you explicitly select them.

The Mac app supports both Apple Silicon and Intel architectures and requires macOS 26 or later.
