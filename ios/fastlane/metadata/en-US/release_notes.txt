Version 1.3 makes private brainstorming more reliable and more portable.

NEW
• Export boards as JPG alongside PNG, SVG, and JSON.
• Customize templates with Apple's on-device Foundation Models.

IMPROVED
• Model readiness is checked more reliably after a restart, system update, or return to the app.
• The availability message now distinguishes unsupported hardware from a model that is still preparing.
• Refreshed app icon and launch artwork.

Your prompts and board context stay on your device when using Apple Foundation Models. Feedback is welcome at luke@lukesteuber.com.
