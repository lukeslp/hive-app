Turn one thought into a visual map of everything around it.

Idea Tiles is a hexagonal brainstorming canvas for writers, designers, product thinkers, researchers, and anyone who works better with connected fragments than a blank page. Start with one tile, explore the ideas around it, and keep following the branches that matter.

EXPLORE THE SPACE AROUND AN IDEA

• Expand a tile into six related directions with Apple Intelligence on supported devices.
• Merge two tiles into one thought that captures what they share.
• Star important themes and filter a busy board down to what matters.
• Undo, redo, pan, and zoom without breaking your flow.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

On supported iPhone and iPad models, generation runs through Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Prompts, board context, and responses stay on your device. There is no silent cloud fallback, no account requirement, no advertising profile, and no analytics SDK in the native app.

MAKE THE BOARD PORTABLE

Export your work as PNG, JPG, SVG, or JSON through the standard share sheet. Send it with AirDrop or Messages, save it to Files, or keep the structured JSON for later editing.

RETURN TO YOUR WORK

Boards are saved locally across sessions. Template customization also uses the on-device model, and model readiness is checked again when the app returns to the foreground.

ACCESSIBLE AND RESPONSIVE

Idea Tiles includes VoiceOver labels, adjustable text, high-contrast options, reduced-motion support, keyboard shortcuts, and touch interactions designed for both iPhone and iPad.

REQUIREMENTS

• Generation requires iOS 26 or later, Apple Intelligence enabled, and eligible hardware.
• Canvas editing, merge, star, filter, local sessions, and export remain available without generation.
• When the system model is unavailable or still preparing, Idea Tiles explains why and checks again automatically.

Idea Tiles is free, has no subscription or in-app purchase, and is open source under the MIT license.
